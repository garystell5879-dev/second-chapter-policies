Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains what information Second Chapter collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to joinsecondchapter.com and related services.

The short version: we use your information to match you with people and run the service. We do not sell your personal information. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

Information You Give Us

  • Account details: name, email address, date of birth, location, and login information.
  • Profile and questionnaire answers: your photos, bio, your 20-question questionnaire responses, and preferences such as your gender and the gender of people you want to meet. Some of these answers, such as your sexual orientation, are sensitive. We use them only to provide matches and operate the Service.
  • Messages: messages you send to your matches.
  • Photo Booth: scene descriptions you write and the AI-generated images created from your profile photos.
  • Reports and support requests you send us.
  • Payment information: handled directly by our payment processor. We receive confirmation of payment and limited details such as the card type and last four digits, but never your full card number.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and browser type, IP address, approximate location, and log data such as pages viewed and times of access.
  • Cookies and similar technologies needed to keep you logged in and the site working.

How We Use Information

  • To create your account and select your daily matches.
  • To enable messaging and the Photo Booth between confirmed matches.
  • To process payments and manage memberships.
  • To keep members safe, investigate reports, and prevent fraud, abuse and illegal activity.
  • To communicate with you about your account, policy changes and support.
  • To improve the Service and the matching system.
  • To comply with the law.

Who Can See Your Information

  • Other members: your profile is shown to members you are matched with. Messages and booth images are visible only to you and the match you share them with.
  • Service providers who help us run the Service, such as database hosting, payment processing, email delivery, and the AI image service used by the Photo Booth. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Law enforcement and safety: when required by law, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is needed to prevent harm, protect someone's safety, or respond to illegal activity. See our Law Enforcement Guidelines.
  • Business transfers: if Second Chapter is sold or merged, information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising by other companies.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or de-identify your profile, questionnaire answers, photos and messages within 30 days, except where we must keep certain records for legal, safety, fraud-prevention or billing reasons. Photo Booth images for a match are deleted for both members when either member unmatches.

Your Choices and Rights

  • You can view and edit your profile at any time.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account by emailing privacy@joinsecondchapter.com.
  • You can opt out of non-essential emails using the unsubscribe link.

We will respond to requests within 30 days and may need to verify your identity first.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Adults Only

The Service is only for people 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and we delete any such account we discover.

Changes

We may update this policy. If changes are significant, we will notify you on the site or by email before they take effect.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: privacy@joinsecondchapter.com.
