Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

In short: no refunds, cancel anytime. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Cancelling

  • You can cancel your weekly or monthly membership at any time from your account settings, or by emailing support@joinsecondchapter.com.
  • Cancellation stops future renewals. Your membership stays active until the end of the current billing period.
  • To avoid the next charge, cancel before your renewal date.

No Refunds

All membership fees and Photo Booth credit purchases are final and non-refundable, including for partially used billing periods, unused matches, and unused credits. Unused Photo Booth credits have no cash value.

Founders

Founders are not charged during their two free months. If a founder does not add payment information when the free period ends, the membership simply ends and no charge is made.

Account Removal

If your account is suspended or terminated for violating our Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy, you are not eligible for a refund.

Billing Errors and Legal Rights

If you were charged in error, such as a duplicate charge or a charge after you cancelled, contact support@joinsecondchapter.com within 30 days and we will correct it. Nothing in this policy limits any refund right you have under applicable law.
