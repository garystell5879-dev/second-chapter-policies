Safety Tips

Effective November 3, 2026

SECOND CHAPTER DOES NOT CONDUCT CRIMINAL BACKGROUND SCREENINGS OF ITS MEMBERS.

Members confirm when they join that they have not been convicted of a violent or sexual offense, but we do not verify this. Even services that do run background checks can't catch everything: not all records are public, databases are not always up to date, and checks usually miss arrests and convictions from other countries. Use the precautions below with everyone you meet.

Before you meet

  • Keep personal details private at first. Don't share your last name, home address, workplace, phone number or daily routine until you know someone well.
  • Watch for fake profiles. People sometimes use stolen photos and invented stories. Be cautious if someone's story doesn't add up, they avoid a video call, or they push to move the conversation off Second Chapter quickly.
  • Never send money. Don't send money, gift cards, cryptocurrency or financial information to anyone you meet here, no matter the reason or how urgent it sounds. Report anyone who asks.
  • Take your time. There's no rush. A video call before meeting is a good way to confirm someone is who they say they are.

When you meet

  • Meet in a public place with other people around, like a busy coffee shop or restaurant. Never meet at your home or theirs the first time.
  • Tell a friend or family member who you're meeting, where, and when you expect to be home. Check in with them afterward.
  • Get yourself there and back. Drive yourself or use your own ride so you can leave whenever you want.
  • Stay clear-headed. Limit alcohol and keep an eye on your drink.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels wrong, it's fine to leave.

If something goes wrong

  • In an emergency, call 911.
  • Use the Report button on a member's profile or in your conversation. Reporting also removes them from your introductions and matches.
  • Email safety@joinsecondchapter.com with any safety concern.
  • National Human Trafficking Hotline: 1-888-373-7888, or text 233733.
