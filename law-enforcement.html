Law Enforcement Guidelines

Effective October 21, 2026

These guidelines are for law enforcement and government agencies seeking information about Second Chapter accounts. Members with safety concerns should contact safety@joinsecondchapter.com or call 911.

How to Submit a Request

Send requests by email to legal@joinsecondchapter.com, or by mail to:

Second Chapter
Attn: Law Enforcement Requests
507 W. Carl Hubbell Blvd
Meeker, Oklahoma 74855

Please include the requesting agency, the officer's name, badge number, official email and phone number, the legal process attached, and the account's email address or profile identifier.

Legal Process Required

We disclose member information only in response to valid legal process issued under U.S. law, such as a subpoena, court order or search warrant, consistent with the Stored Communications Act. The content of messages generally requires a search warrant. We review each request and may reject requests that are overly broad or legally deficient.

Emergency Requests

If there is an emergency involving imminent risk of death or serious physical injury, we may disclose information without legal process. Send emergency requests to legal@joinsecondchapter.com with "EMERGENCY" in the subject line and describe the nature of the emergency.

Preservation Requests

We will preserve available account records for 90 days upon receiving an official preservation request, and may extend the preservation once on a renewed request while formal legal process is obtained.

Information We May Have

  • Basic account information, such as name, email address, date of birth and account creation date.
  • Profile content, photos and questionnaire responses.
  • Messages between matched members and Photo Booth images, while they have not been deleted.
  • IP addresses and login records.
  • Limited payment records. Full card numbers are held by our payment processor, not by us.

Deleted content may not be recoverable. Photo Booth images are deleted when a match ends.

Notice to Members

Our policy is to notify members of requests for their information before disclosure, unless we are legally prohibited from doing so or the case involves an emergency, child exploitation, or a risk of harm.

Proactive Reporting

We report apparent child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) as required by law, and we report suspected human trafficking and credible threats of violence to the appropriate authorities.
