Acceptable Use Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Second Chapter exists to help adults find genuine, respectful connections. This policy describes what is not allowed on the Service. It is part of our Terms of Service. Violations can lead to removal of content, suspension or permanent termination of your account, and reports to law enforcement.

Zero Tolerance

The following result in immediate, permanent removal and, where appropriate, a report to law enforcement:

  • Human trafficking of any kind, including sex or labor trafficking, or recruiting, transporting or controlling any person through force, fraud or coercion.
  • Sexual exploitation, including offering, soliciting or arranging commercial sex, escort services, prostitution or "sugar" arrangements.
  • Any content, request or conduct involving minors in a sexual or exploitative way, or any attempt by a minor to use the Service.
  • Aiding, abetting, encouraging or threatening abuse or violence against any person.

Respect for Other Members

  • No harassment, bullying, stalking, threats or intimidation.
  • No hate speech or content attacking people based on race, ethnicity, religion, national origin, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or age.
  • No unwanted sexual messages or explicit images.
  • No sharing another person's private information, photos or messages without their consent.
  • Do not contact a member who has unmatched or blocked you through other means.

Honesty

  • Use your own real photos and accurate information about yourself.
  • Do not impersonate anyone or create fake or duplicate accounts.
  • Do not use the Service if you are married or in a committed relationship unless that is honestly disclosed.

Scams and Money

  • Never ask other members for money, gifts, cryptocurrency, investments, banking details or financial help.
  • No romance scams, phishing, or schemes to move conversations off the Service in order to defraud someone.
  • No advertising, selling, recruiting or commercial promotion of any kind.

Photo Booth Rules

  • No nudity, sexual content, violence, weapons used against people, or illegal activity in booth descriptions or images.
  • Images may show only the two matched members. Do not attempt to depict minors, celebrities or any other real person.
  • Do not remove the Second Chapter watermark or present booth images as real photographs.
  • Do not use booth images to embarrass, threaten or blackmail anyone.

Protecting the Service

  • No hacking, scraping, bots, automated accounts, or attempts to get around our security or matching limits.
  • No uploading malware or interfering with the Service's operation.
  • No use of the Service in violation of any law.

Reporting

If you see anything that breaks these rules, use the report option in the app or email safety@joinsecondchapter.com. Reports are confidential. If anyone is in immediate danger, call 911. To report suspected human trafficking, you can also contact the National Human Trafficking Hotline at 1-888-373-7888 (text 233733).

Enforcement

We review reports and may remove content, issue warnings, suspend or terminate accounts, and preserve and share information with law enforcement as described in our Law Enforcement Guidelines and Privacy Policy. Accounts removed for violations are not eligible for refunds.
